# Supermart_Grocery_Sales_Dashboard
## Project Objective
The main objective of the Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard is to analyze the company’s sales data and create an interactive, easy-to-understand dashboard that helps management quickly monitor and evaluate business performance.
## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/singhdarshan3417-arch/Amazon-Sales-Data-India/blob/main/Amazon%20Sales%20Data%20India.ods">Dataset</a>

## Questions
1. What are the total sales and total profit?
2. Which product categories contribute the largest share of total sales?
3. How do sales change over time?
4. Which regions generate the highest sales?
5.  Which categories and sub-categories generate the highest sales?
6.   hich products have high sales but comparatively low profit?
7.   Which categories and regions generate the highest profit?
8.   Is there a relationship between discount and profit?
9.   Which states and cities generate the highest sales?
10.   Which locations have high sales but comparatively low profit?

## Final conclusion:
The Supermart Sales Dashboard converts raw sales data into actionable business insights by identifying sales and profit trends, strong and weak-performing categories and regions, and the relationship between discounts and profitability. It enables management to make faster and more informed decisions for improving overall business performance
